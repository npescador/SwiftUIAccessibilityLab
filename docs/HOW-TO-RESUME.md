## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Always mention the path** - Even if you think I remember
2. **Name specific documents** - Don't say "read the docs", say which ones
3. **State your phase** - Where are you in the checklist?
4. **Be specific about action** - What do you want help with RIGHT NOW?
